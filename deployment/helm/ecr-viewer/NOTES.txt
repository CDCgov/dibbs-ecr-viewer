## Database Setup:

This chart requires an external database. Set the DATABASE_URL in your values file:

values-dev.yaml:
  database:
    url: "postgresql://user:password@localhost:5432/ecr_dev"

values-prod.yaml:
  database:
    url: "postgresql://user:password@prod-db:5432/ecr_prod"
